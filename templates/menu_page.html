About The Vault

A campus marketplace made by Hampton students, for Hampton students.

What is it?

The Vault is where students sell and shop clothing right here on campus. If you've got your own brand, you can open a storefront and list your stuff. If you're shopping, you can browse brands, save what you like, and message the seller.

How it works

  1. Make an account with your school email.
  2. Open a storefront and list your items, or browse what's already up.
  3. Check out, then meet the seller on campus to finish the exchange.

Meetups only

Everything is an in-person exchange. We don't take payments on the site, you and the seller sort that out when you meet up.

Rate your favorites

Shopped with a store? Give it 1 to 5 stars so other students know who's worth it. The highest rated stores show up on the Top Rated page.
