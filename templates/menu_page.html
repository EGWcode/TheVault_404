About The Vault

The Vault is a campus marketplace for Hampton University students. Student-run clothing brands can open a storefront, list their items, and sell to other students.

Everything is an in-person meetup. You pick your items, check out, and meet the seller on campus to complete the exchange. No payments are taken on the site.
